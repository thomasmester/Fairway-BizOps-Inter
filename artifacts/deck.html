Part 1 · How much to trust it

The order holds when the weights move; the sample is the weak point

Sensitivity: 11 weight scenarios

Each weight moved ±10 points, the others rescaled.

  • 8 states make the top 10 in every scenario
  • California is in 10 of 11, Virginia in 9 of 11
  • Arkansas swaps in 3 times; nothing else does

Data limits

  • Only 5 days of transactions, including a weekend
  • One customer is 68% of the sample
  • Rep probabilities are self-reported
  • Forms per state assumed to be 2

Sensitivity from analysis.py; the Excel Weights tab re-ranks live

The ranking is robust to reasonable disagreement about the weights. What could really change it is better data: a longer transaction history and the actual form count per state.

Part 2 · Feedback into mappings

Seven steps; nothing changes until it's verified and scoped

1 · Capture

Log every comment against form, field, transaction and customer

2 · Classify

Mapping, condition, preference, extraction, form version or conflict

3 · Verify

Check the DMV rule and the source documents first

4 · Scope

Global state rule, or a per-customer override?

5 · Change

Edit the mapping record, with the reason and a link to the comment

6 · Test

Regression-test on past transactions for the form

7 · Close the loop

Reply to the customer; track the repeat-error rate per form

The full triage of all six example comments is in the workbook's Feedback Process tab

The core idea: feedback is a signal, not an instruction. Some comments are mapping errors, some are extraction bugs, some are customer preferences, and some are wrong. Verifying and scoping stop one clerk's habit from becoming a global rule.

Part 2 · The six examples

Only half of them are mapping edits

CommentCategoryWhat we do
1 · Lessor, not lesseeMapping errorRepoint the field globally; retest leases
2 · Blank odometer if newRule or preferenceState exempts it: global rule. Else a customer override
3 · VIN has O for 0Extraction bugSend to AI team; add VIN validation. No mapping edit
4 · Old form versionForm versionVersion the form, re-map, recalibrate; watch DMVs
5 · Dealer fees, yes or noConflictingHold; check the state's price definition; one answer from the admin
6 · Lien on a cash saleCondition errorFix the "only if financed" rule, or the input data

Mapping or condition edits: 1, 6 and possibly 2. The rest go to extraction, forms or the customer.

Point out example 3: a VIN never contains O, I or Q, so this can be caught automatically with a validation rule. Example 5 is the most important process point: conflicting feedback never goes straight into a mapping.

Before committing

What I'd want to know

  1. How many forms each state really needs, and can mappings be reused across states?
  2. Is 5 days representative? I'd want 3–6 months per customer and state
  3. Which states would customer 87 move to Autofill first under D01?
  4. Hard dates: the D09 Q4 deadline, the D03 POC scoring, D07's go-live need
  5. Can Audit's existing state rules speed up calibration?
  6. Are any DMV form changes pending in the top 10 states?
Close by saying the plan is designed to be revisited after wave 1: by day 11 we'll have real Autofill usage and feedback to re-rank with.
