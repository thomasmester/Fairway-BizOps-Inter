Part 2 · The six examples

Only half of them are mapping edits

CommentCategoryWhat we do
1 · Lessor, not lesseeMapping errorRepoint the field globally; retest leases

3 · VIN has O for 0Extraction bugSend to AI team; add VIN validation. No mapping edit
4 · Old form versionForm versionVersion the form, re-map, recalibrate; watch DMVs
5 · Dealer fees, yes or noConflictingHold; check the state's price definition; one answer from the admin
6 · Lien on a cash saleCondition errorFix the "only if financed" rule, or the input data

Mapping or condition edits: 1, 6 and possibly 2. The rest go to extraction, forms or the customer.

Point out example 3: a VIN never contains O, I or Q, so this can be caught automatically with a validation rule. Example 5 is the most important process point: conflicting feedback never goes straight into a mapping.
