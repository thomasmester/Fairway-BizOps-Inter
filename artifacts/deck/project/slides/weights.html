Part 1 · How the data was weighed

85% of the score comes from customers we already have

45% · Existing-customer volume

The state's share of the 200 recent transactions

20% · Anchor customer 87

Our biggest account's transactions in the state

20% · Commitments

TX-first ask (D02); signed Q4 promise (D09)

10% · Autofill pipeline

Value x probability x haircut, Autofill deals only

5% · Market size

FHWA registrations + 2025 new-vehicle sales

Each component is scaled 0–1 against the top state, then weighted. All weights are editable in the Excel model.

Why a separate anchor component: volume alone over-indexes on TX and FL, where customers 110 and 121 file. Customer 87 brings us the most business elsewhere, so their states (AL, CO, GA) get lifted. Market size is 5% because it describes long-run TAM, not which form to calibrate this week. Market data: FHWA Highway Statistics 2023, Table MV-1, and F&I Tools 2025 new-vehicle sales by state.
